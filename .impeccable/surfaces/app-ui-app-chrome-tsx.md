## Scope
Whole Spinbox app shell plus Now playing, browse, playlists, settings, invites and auth. Mode: Operate. Household listeners on couch phones, desktop browsers and a shared TV.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An evening's listening is a printed programme. The record turns on a night-blue deck, and the queue is a timetable of when each track will air. It refuses the streaming-clone layout of dark artwork grid plus a bottom bar.
OWN-WORLD: Night #16204A/#1D2A5E deck and rail, paper #F2F1EC pages, ink #111318, hairline rules #D3D2CB, ON AIR red #E5322D for the live state only, highlight yellow #F2C230 for the current row only. Archivo condensed uppercase for headings and times, normal width for body, tabular figures. Flat ink, no gradients or glows, square corners, colour quarantined to artwork plates.
STORY: The household sees what is on now and what airs next, at what time, and can put the next record on with one tap.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Now playing (desktop): night rail at the left, the record with tonearm and ON AIR tab large on the deck, a condensed title, icon transport and a flat seek rule; at the right, "Tonight's listing" with projected times, current row in yellow. Phone: the deck owns the full screen with no frame, and the listing sits below.
FORM: Radio listings magazine ("Tonight's Programme"), #7 on my ordered list, seed key 4759ddd2. Signature interaction: projected on-air times that update in place as the playhead moves; recently played shows "earlier tonight" times.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
